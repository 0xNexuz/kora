# Problem

## One-line problem

African SMEs cannot reliably turn fragmented cash, payment, invoice, supplier, and repayment activity into timely decisions and contextual working capital because the evidence is split across operational systems and informal workflows.

## Users and current process

- Primary: owner-operators and finance leads at African SMEs.
- Secondary: capital providers and auditors reviewing permissioned evidence.
- Today: spreadsheets, bank/POS portals, invoice tools, messages, and manual reconciliation.
- Breaks: cash timing is hard to see, scenario assumptions are not reproducible, and financing is detached from the operating context.

## Root cause

The business lacks one permissioned financial state model that joins operating evidence, deterministic analysis, authorization, settlement, and repayment history.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting software | Records transactions | Usually not an evidence-backed operating/capital loop |
| Lending application | Provides capital | Starts with a loan request rather than operating context |
| Generic AI dashboard | Conversational UX | May invent calculations or lack traceable evidence |

## Success condition

A system must transform permissioned business evidence into reproducible cash-flow analysis, compare non-debt and financing scenarios, require approval, settle an appropriate agreement, and feed verified repayment back into business history.

## Non-goals

- Trading, investments, or generic crypto portfolio management.
- Statutory accounting, autonomous lending, or mainnet consumer finance in this MVP.
- Claiming real customers, prediction accuracy, or creditworthiness from fictional data.

## Validation

Product-problem validation from real user research is **UNVERIFIED**. The current build validates technical feasibility with a fictional Nigerian pharmacy.
